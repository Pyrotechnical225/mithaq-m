# Mithaq — Meet haq in marriage

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Muslim matrimony / matchmaking platform
- **What they do:** Private, structured introduction service for practicing Muslims with family & imam involvement, prioritizing deen-aligned compatibility over browsing.
- **Target audience:** Marriage-minded Muslim professionals seeking serious introductions with family support
- **Page goal:** Establish trust in the process & convert to profile signup

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Left-aligned layout: H1 "A serious way to find a spouse." (large display type, tight letter-spacing) sits above 2 paragraphs of body copy. Primary CTA "Start your profile" (white text on dark forest-green background, arrow icon) sits left of secondary link "See the process" (dark text, no background). Below: 3 inline checkmarks (✓ No swiping · ✓ Private profiles · ✓ Imam-supported) in small secondary text. Right side: card panel labeled "THE MITHAQ PROCESS" with numbered list (01–04) of 4 steps, light background, sits flush at right edge. Hero background is off-white. Accent color (forest green) used only in primary CTA button.

### Header / Navigation
Horizontal static navigation, left logo "Mithaq" + Arabic script, center 3 nav links (Our principles · How it works · Safety), right secondary link "Sign in" + primary CTA "Get started" (dark text on dark forest-green background, no outline). No hamburger; full horizontal layout.

### Primary CTA / Input
"Start your profile" button (dark forest-green background, white text, arrow glyph) appears twice: hero (left-aligned) and again as right-aligned link in "How it works" section. Action: navigate to `/auth` signup. Button styling consistent across page.

### "A supervised path to an introduction" (process carousel / sidebar card)
- **Purpose:** Enumerate 4-step process before deep-dive sections; establish progression clarity.
- **Layout:** Right-aligned card panel in hero. Numbered list (01–04), each item = step title + 1-line descriptor. Single footer line: "Private by default, with wali and family involvement welcomed." Light background, lightly rounded corners.

### "Our principles" section (3 numbered articles ×1)
- **Purpose:** Communicate platform values & differentiation.
- **Layout:** Full-width section, off-white background. H2 "Marriage deserves a careful beginning." left-aligned, followed by 1 paragraph descriptor. Below: 3 articles in single column; each article = 2-digit number (01, 02, 03) + H3 heading + 1 paragraph. No image; text-only.

### "How it works" section (4 step blocks ×1)
- **Purpose:** Detailed walkthrough of signup & matching workflow.
- **Layout:** Full-width section, off-white background. H2 "One clear step at a time." left-aligned + right-aligned "Start your profile" link. Below: **uniform grid, 4 equal columns**, each cell = 2-digit number + H3 title + 1 paragraph. Columns do not alternate image side (text-only).

### "Safety and dignity" section
- **Purpose:** Privacy & safety commitments.
- **Layout:** Full-width section, dark forest-green background (high contrast). Left: H2 "Private where it matters." + 1 paragraph (white text). Right: 4 checkmark bullets (white text, checkmark glyph), each 1 line. Columns approximately equal width.

### "Begin with sincere intention." section (CTA footer)
- **Purpose:** Final conversion call before footer.
- **Layout:** Center-aligned. H2 + 1 paragraph + centered "Start your profile" button (dark forest-green). Arabic script "ميثاق" centered above heading. Off-white background.

### Footer
- **Purpose:** Site links & legal.
- **Layout:** 3-column layout. Left: logo + 1 paragraph (site descriptor). Center: "About" column (4 links). Right: "Learn" column (4 links). Bottom: copyright + right-aligned "Private matchmaking. Imam-supported introductions." Footer background light gray.

**Notable patterns:** 
- Primary CTA (forest-green button) appears ×4 (hero, header, "How it works", final section).
- Process steps appear twice: hero sidebar (compact 4-item list) & full "How it works" section (4-column uniform grid).
- No image alternation; all sections text or solid-background cards.
- Dark forest-green background used only in "Safety and dignity" section & buttons; off-white section backgrounds throughout.
- Lightly rounded corners on all cards & buttons.
- 2-column grid (hero left / sidebar right); shifts to single column or uniform grids in body sections.
