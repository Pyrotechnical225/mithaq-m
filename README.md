# Halal Union

can you create a website that is purposefully for arranging and matching people for halal marriages:

First make the name for the website "Mithaq" written in arabic and with a tagline like "Building homes, the halal way." and a description like "Mithaq honors marriage as the sacred covenant it is described as in Islam. Our platform matches practicing Muslims based on religious commitment, family values, and life goals, with built-in respect for wali involvement and Islamic marriage practices — helping you begin your journey the halal way." 

Also on the front page put a big button saying "Start searching". when you press that button it will bring you to a survey with fifty question regarding information about you and want you want from your future spouse.

The fifty questions will be :

Required Questions (1–30)

Basic Information

What is your age? — Free text (numeric)

What is your gender? — Male / Female

What is your current city/country of residence? — Free text

Are you willing to relocate for marriage? — Yes / No / Depends on location / Not sure

What is your nationality/ethnic background? — Free text

What is your marital status? — Never married / Divorced / Widowed / Other

Do you have children? — No children / 1–2 children / 3+ children / Prefer not to say

What is your highest level of education? — High school / Bachelor's degree / Master's or Doctorate / Other

What is your current occupation/field of work? — Free text

Religious Practice
10. What school of thought (madhab) do you follow? — Hanafi / Shafi'i / Maliki or Hanbali / Other / No specific madhab
11. How would you describe your level of religious practice? — Very practicing / Moderately practicing / Still learning / Cultural Muslim
12. How often do you pray the five daily prayers? — All five daily / Most of them / Occasionally / Rarely
13. Do you attend Jummah/mosque regularly? — Always / Often / Rarely / Never
14. Do you keep a beard (men) or wear hijab/niqab (women)? — Yes / No / Sometimes / Other
15. How important is it that your spouse matches your level of religious practice? — Very important / Somewhat important / Not important / Open, as long as they're respectful
16. Do you strictly follow a halal diet? — Yes, always / Mostly / Occasionally lenient / Other
17. What is your stance on alcohol in your household? — Strictly none / Prefer none but flexible / Not a major concern / Other
18. Do you read/recite the Quran regularly? — Daily / Weekly / Occasionally / Rarely

Marriage Intentions
19. What is your intended timeline for marriage? — Within 6 months / 6–12 months / 1–2 years / Not sure / Open
20. Will a wali (guardian) be involved in your marriage process? — Yes, required / Yes, preferred / No / Other
21. What are your expectations regarding mahr (dowry)? — Free text
22. Are you open to marrying someone with children from a previous marriage? — Yes / No / Open to it, depending on circumstances / Not sure
23. Have you been previously engaged or in a formal marriage process before? — Yes / No / Prefer not to say

Family & Practical Matters
24. How involved is your family in your marriage decision? — Very involved / Somewhat involved / Minimal involvement / Fully independent
25. Do you smoke or use tobacco products? — Yes / No / Occasionally / Trying to quit
26. Do you have any health conditions your potential spouse should know about? — Free text
27. What language(s) do you speak fluently? — Free text
28. What is your current financial situation? — Entry-level income / Moderate income / High income / Prefer not to say
29. Do you have any outstanding debts that could affect marriage planning? — No debt / Minor, manageable debt / Significant debt / Prefer not to say
30. What are you looking for in a spouse? — Free text

Optional Questions (31–50)

Personality & Lifestyle
31. How would you describe your personality? — Introverted / Extroverted / Balanced / Other
32. What are your hobbies and interests? — Free text
33. How do you prefer to spend your free time? — Staying home / Socializing/outings / Outdoor activities / Other
34. How would you describe your sense of humor? — Witty/sarcastic / Lighthearted/silly / Dry/subtle / Other
35. How do you typically handle stress or conflict? — Talk it through calmly / Need space first / Seek advice from others / Other

Family & Children
36. How many children would you like to have? — None / 1–2 / 3+ / Open/undecided
37. What parenting style do you envision? — Strict/traditional / Balanced / Relaxed/flexible / Other
38. How do you envision your relationship with in-laws? — Very close, living near/together / Regular contact, separate homes / Minimal involvement / Other
39. Where do you see yourself living long-term? — Own independent home / With/near extended family / Open to either / Other
40. What role do you envision for division of household chores? — Traditional roles / Shared equally / Flexible based on circumstance / Other

Values & Expectations
41. How do you prefer to handle financial decisions? — Jointly / One partner primarily manages / Kept separate / Other
42. What is your love language? — Words of affirmation / Quality time / Acts of service / Other
43. How important is your spouse's career ambition to you? — Very important / Somewhat important / Not important / No preference
44. What are your views on your spouse continuing education/career after marriage? — Full support to continue / Support, with family as priority / Prefer focus on home life / Other

Islamic & Community Life
45. Are you involved in Islamic community activities or organizations? — Yes, actively / Occasionally / Not currently / Other
46. Do you follow any particular Islamic scholars or speakers? — Free text
47. How important is it to raise children with strong Islamic education? — Very important / Somewhat important / Open to spouse's input / Not a priority
48. What are your views on Islamic vs. civil marriage documentation? — Nikah only / Both nikah and civil / Civil first, then nikah / Other

Compatibility Extras

50. Is there anything else you'd like a potential match to know? — Free text
